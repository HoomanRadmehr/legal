## Product context

Build an internal Legal Management Module that is both:

- a system of record for legal information; and
- a system of deadlines for critical legal obligations.

The system must be secure, organized, auditable, and suitable for confidential legal work.
Persian date handling is required where relevant.

## Required MVP capabilities

- JWT authentication and clear role boundaries for Legal Admin, Legal Manager, Legal Counsel / Case Lead, and Viewer.
- Legal case management.
- Contract management.
- Legal notice intake and response tracking.
- Deadline views for upcoming, overdue, today, and assigned-to-me.
- Tasks linked to legal matters.
- Private documents with record-based access control.
- Activity log / audit trail.
- Permission-aware dashboard.
- Ownership reassignment and an offboarding flow.
- Setup instructions, migrations/schema, seed data, tests, README, and `AI_USAGE.md`.

## Evaluation priorities

The design should visibly demonstrate:

- confidentiality and legal-domain understanding;
- sensible architecture and data modeling;
- effective AI-assisted workflow with human verification;
- readable, secure, modular code;
- tests for core logic; and
- disciplined prioritization within a 1-2 day timebox.
